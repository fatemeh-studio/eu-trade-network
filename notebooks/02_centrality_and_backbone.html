02 · Centrality & backbone

RQ1 — who is central, and where does Austria rank? Computes degree, strength, betweenness, eigenvector centrality and PageRank on the directed weighted graph, ranks the hubs through SQL, and extracts the disparity-filter backbone.

Why weighted betweenness uses distance = 1 / value_kusd, and why unweighted betweenness is identically zero here, is explained on the Data & method page.

aut = hubs.loc[hubs["iso3"] == "AUT"].iloc[0]
aut_pr_rank = int(
    hubs.sort_values("pagerank", ascending=False)
    .reset_index(drop=True)
    .assign(r=lambda d: d.index + 1)
    .loc[lambda d: d["iso3"] == "AUT", "r"]
    .iloc[0]
)
aut_str_rank = int(
    cent.sort_values("out_strength", ascending=False)
    .reset_index(drop=True)
    .assign(r=lambda d: d.index + 1)
    .loc[lambda d: d["iso3"] == "AUT", "r"]
    .iloc[0]
)
n_zero_b = int((hubs["betweenness"] == 0).sum())
n_pos_b = int((hubs["betweenness"] > 0).sum())
top_hub = hubs.iloc[0]

display(
    Markdown(
        f"**{top_hub['name']} leads, and position is not volume.** "
        f"{top_hub['name']} ({top_hub['iso3']}) scores "
        f"{float(top_hub['betweenness']):.3f} on weighted betweenness. Only {n_pos_b} of "
        f"{len(hubs)} economies score above zero at all, because the digraph is complete "
        f"(density = {summary['density']:.2f}): for most pairs the direct link is already "
        f"the cheapest path, so nobody sits between them.\n\n"
        f"**Austria is not a bridge.** It is one of the {n_zero_b} economies with "
        f"betweenness = 0 — the SQL rank of {int(aut['rank'])} is an arbitrary tie-break "
        f"among those zeros, not a ranking. Its partners already trade with each other "
        f"directly. By export strength Austria is {aut_str_rank}th of {len(hubs)} and by "
        f"PageRank {aut_pr_rank}th: a mid-tier EU economy whose largest partner is Germany "
        f"on both sides of the trade balance."
    )
)

Germany leads, and position is not volume. Germany (DEU) scores 0.829 on weighted betweenness. Only 15 of 35 economies score above zero at all, because the digraph is complete (density = 1.00): for most pairs the direct link is already the cheapest path, so nobody sits between them.

Austria is not a bridge. It is one of the 20 economies with betweenness = 0 — the SQL rank of 35 is an arbitrary tie-break among those zeros, not a ranking. Its partners already trade with each other directly. By export strength Austria is 17th of 35 and by PageRank 13th: a mid-tier EU economy whose largest partner is Germany on both sides of the trade balance.

Disparity-filter backbone

Statistically significant edges at config.DISPARITY_ALPHA (Serrano, Boguñá & Vespignani 2009). Also compute the rich-club coefficient on the undirected projection.

Show code 
backbone = metrics.disparity_filter(G, alpha=config.DISPARITY_ALPHA)
bb_edges = pd.DataFrame(
    [
        {"exporter_iso3": u, "importer_iso3": v, "value_kusd": float(d["weight"])}
        for u, v, d in backbone.edges(data=True)
    ]
)
rc = metrics.rich_club(G)

print(
    f"Backbone keeps {backbone.number_of_edges()} / {G.number_of_edges()} edges "
    f"at alpha={config.DISPARITY_ALPHA}"
)
rc.tail(10)
